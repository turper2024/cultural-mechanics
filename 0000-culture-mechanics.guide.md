# Culture Mechanics Knowledge Guide

> A compact, LLM-oriented map for selecting and interpreting Culture Mechanics
> sources. It is a navigation aid, not an authoritative theory definition.

## Status and use

Use this guide to translate an ordinary-language question into the smallest relevant
source set. The user does not need to know Culture Mechanics terminology. Infer
likely concepts from the problem as described, then verify them against the source.

The six semantic documents named below are authoritative for this project. Their
definitions and distinctions override this compressed guide. Culture Mechanics and
its extensions are proposed by this project; do not present them as disciplinary
consensus. Keep claims made by the framework distinct from external evidence and
from conclusions inferred while applying it to a case.

## Practical implementation authority

For exact culture-implementation practice, the definitive source is [Ian
Macdonald, Catherine Burke, and Karl Stewart's *Systems Leadership: Creating
Positive
Organisations*](https://www.routledge.com/Systems-Leadership-Creating-Positive-Organisations/MacDonald-Burke-Stewart/p/book/9781138036574).
The originating framework judges its method approximately 99% correct as a
qualitative verdict. It coordinates leadership behaviour, systems, symbols,
mythologies, habits,
organizational arrangements, and repeated experience; do not reduce it to
mythology alone.

*Mythology Construction* isolates one elevated layer: mythologies as the
**assembly language of the bigOther**. It studies two substantive paths:
**endogenous mythology formation**, the distributed plant-like growth and
selection of mythology, and **purposive mythology construction**, an open research
possibility concerning intentional design and release. Its present workflow is a
provisional research and design scaffold, not yet the complete purposive lifecycle
and not a whole-culture implementation method. Systems, symbols, habits, and
conduct may be equally necessary or more decisive. If the book is unavailable
when exact implementation is requested, identify the missing external source
rather than reconstructing its method.

## Constitutive commitments

Preserve these commitments whenever the theory is applied:

1. **Functionally non-unitary homo duplex.** The main CPU and the local bigOther are
   distinct components of the model. The main CPU reasons, compares, imagines, and
   plans. The bigOther applies culturally learned classifications and sentiments to
   thoughts, persons, sources, roles, actions, symbols, and events. Do not silently
   reduce the bigOther to ordinary cognition, a generic bias, System 1, the
   superego, or internalized norms.
2. **Emergent collective-field back-action.** Networked local bigOthers can produce
   mechanical solidarity, a distributed bigOther, and, under sufficiently
   integrated conditions, a multi-mind-self. These are implemented through persons
   and communication, but the framework does not treat them as adequately explained
   by adding together independently formed individual psychologies. The resulting
   field acts back upon participants through meanings, authorities, expectations,
   institutions, rewards, and sanctions.
3. **Single-slot exclusivity with package compositionality.** One local bigOther
   occupies the one PCI slot and executes one operative mythological lens. The lens
   may braid several compatible religious, professional, familial, national, or
   local packages; these are constituent strands, not independent bigOthers ranked
   at runtime. Paul's pride in supporting himself through work illustrates how
   Christianity and a *Berufsethos* can reinforce one another. Christianity and
   the National Socialist worldview are treated by the originating framework as
   deeply incompatible programming systems: Nazi capture required an incumbent
   Christian braid to be
   unbraided and one Nazi-governed successor to be rebraided, not installed beside
   an intact Christian program. Retained Christian material receives its operative
   meaning from the successor weave. A counterfield can reinforce, repair, attack,
   or help replace the one installed weave; it does not provide a second PCI card.
   Both a first-order program and a lifted meta-program produce complete slot
   occupancy or **full capture**. First-order mechanical solidarity shares concrete
   rules and answers. Lifted mechanical solidarity shares the rules that generate,
   select, apply, and revise concrete answers. Its generator can govern universally
   while producing plural, bounded first-order outputs and jurisdictions. Keep
   order level, occupancy, jurisdiction, and corrigibility distinct. A rival
   replacement is not pathological merely because it is rival; correction failure,
   interpretive monopoly, epistemic sovereignty, or jurisdictional spillover is.
   Treat the commitment as defeasible: stable same-domain operation by two
   incompatible evaluators that remain independently final, affectively alive, and
   mutually ungoverned would count against strict single-slot exclusivity.
4. **Inspectability.** Distinguish definitions, proposed mechanisms, hypotheses,
   external evidence, analogies, moral or theoretical verdicts, examples, boundary
   conditions, counterinterpretations, and open questions. An application is an
   inference from theory and case facts, not proof of either.

## Core causal architecture

The basic individual-level sequence is:

```text
mythologies + symbols + rituals + institutions + experience
                              ↓
                    mythological lens
                              ↓
local bigOther classifies a thought, source, person, action, or event
                              ↓
felt honour/shame, loyalty/betrayal, legitimacy/illegitimacy,
sacredness/pollution, belonging/exclusion
                              ↓
attention, motivation, judgment, and action of the main CPU are redirected
```

The general weave becomes locally executable through **situated cultural
scripts**:

```text
operative cultural weave
+ role, institution, relationship, or recurrent situation
+ practical and material environment
→ distributed higher-resolution script
→ concrete enactment, consequence, and feedback
```

A script is narrower but more detailed than the cultural braid and can be larger
than one participant. It is not another bigOther. Peripheral scripts may be
repaired or replaced within the incumbent culture; failure of load-bearing or
mutually dependent scripts can form a cross-script cascade and contribute to
cultural unbraiding.

The **relevant-structure appraisal hypothesis** specifies one important,
non-exclusive path through this sequence. People often experience represented support
for a morally weighty relationship, role-context, institution, practice, legal
order, or shared future as good, and represented damage as evil. Keep felt
relevance, represented effect, actual multidimensional effect, and normative
standing separate. This mechanism can explain support for an institution that
strengthens its own continuity or capacity while damaging truth-contact,
corrigibility, subjecthood, or other structures.
The proposed **change-indexed moral-mobilization hypothesis** adds that appraisal
may become especially action-directing when a condition is represented as active
continuation, prevented transition, or culpable omission; it does not define all
ethics or make an unopposed status quo good.

The programming material can arise or be redirected through two routes:

```text
endogenous formation:
changing conditions → receiver–order mismatch → articulation
→ distributed selection → embodiment

prospective purposive construction:
chosen destination D* → baseline and receiver diagnosis
→ mythology and supporting-system intervention → testing and correction
```

Mythology is a privileged symbolic intervention surface, not the whole
culture-implementation stack. The actual successor is jointly constrained by
intention, receiver fit, incumbent and rival fields, institutions, unintended
consequences, and reality.

The doctrinal-progeny test adds a retrospective audit:

```text
explicit doctrine + implicit authority and correction grammar
× receivers + interpreters + institutions + power + conditions
→ descendant distribution → offspring profile
→ defeasible evidence about generative spirit
```

Distinguish entailment, authorization or affordance, selection, concealed causal
design, and appropriation or inversion. *Herrschaftsideologie* names the narrower
case in which claimed privileged access to a hidden truth or collective interest
is converted into governing jurisdiction.

The ideological-symbol interface can make that local state publicly legible:

```text
installed ideological program
              ↓ activated and displayed through
ideological symbol or conformity signal
              ↓
performance, absence, refusal, or substitution becomes observable
              ↓
apparent consensus, recruitment, character accusation, sanction,
or a claim to wider jurisdiction
```

The single-slot composition and replacement constraint is:

```text
one PCI slot → one local bigOther → one operative mythological weave
                                      ↑
                 several compatible packages may be braided within it

incompatible governing offer
        ↓
rejection OR incumbent loosening and unbraiding
        ↓
liminal instability and rebraiding of retained + new strands
        ↓
one successor weave installed; no parallel evaluator or runtime ranking
```

Lifting is a replacement process within the same constraint:

```text
full first-order capture
shared concrete rules and answers are binding as such
                              ↓
unbraid the finality of those concrete answers
                              ↓
rebraid a shared generator
fixed meta-rules M + current inputs K_t + situation C
                              ↓
full lifted capture
the shared generator produces binding first-order output R_t(C)
```

Here *fixed* means installed and binding, not eternal. Generator and output belong
to one weave. Many detailed, self-compulsorily followed prescriptions strongly
indicate first-order solidarity, but detail is not the definition; lifted
generators can also produce extensive concrete rules. Pluralism, division of
labour, and bounded subordinate jurisdictions are possible results of lifting, not
its definition.

The basic collective sequence is:

```text
local bigOthers + shared symbolic protocol + reciprocal signalling
                              ↓
synchronization and mechanical-solidarity field / distributed bigOther
                              ↓
collective meanings, authority, expectations, institutions, and sanctions
                              ↓
back-action upon individual classification, judgment, and behaviour
                              ↓
reproduction, correction, resistance, capture, or change of the field
```

Keep two further collective axes independent. **Cultural interoperability** asks
whether differentiated groups can translate important meanings, recognize common
procedures and jurisdictions, and coordinate required common action; operative
shared lifted solidarity supplies its principal culture-mechanical implementation.
**Subjecthood absorption (fusion)** asks what legitimate standing personal
judgment, conscience, purpose, property, loyalty, identity, relationships, refusal,
and exit retain against the collective. High interoperability can preserve
differentiated unity or become absorptive. Clockwork coordination has an affinity
with differentiated unity; bounded laser synchronization can serve it;
generalized, compulsory, cross-jurisdiction laser synchronization creates fusion
pressure but is not itself proof of fusion.

These are routing summaries only. Use the complete source for qualifications and
for the framework's distinctions between programming, activation, coupling,
emergence, agency, and capture.

## Application rules

- Search semantically across the user's language and the English source language.
  Translate the problem into candidate concepts internally; do not require theory
  vocabulary from the user.
- Begin with a plain-language account, then introduce only the terms that make the
  causal explanation more precise.
- Make mechanisms do explanatory work. Do not write a generic social-psychological
  answer and merely attach Culture Mechanics labels to it.
- Consult the analogy inventory proactively when a listed model sharpens reasoning,
  even if the user did not request an analogy. Use it as a controlled scaffold:
  identify its target, map only the relevant relations, draw only its licensed
  inference, and test its limits or counterexamples. Pair it with the construct's
  primary source; surface the analogy only when it helps the reader. Resemblance is
  not evidence and does not redefine the construct.
- Separate individual processing, interpersonal signalling, institutional
  organization, and emergent collective-field effects when the case spans them.
- Keep cultural interoperability and subjecthood absorption independent. Do not
  infer fusion from common meaning or high synchrony alone, and do not infer
  society-wide interoperability from intense local coherence. Use *clockwork
  coordination*, not *clockwork synchronization*; distinguish bounded laser
  synchronization from generalized and jurisdictionalized laser synchronization.
- Classify ideological symbols by function rather than material form or religious
  versus secular origin. Treat performance, absence, imposition, and attributed
  labels as possible routes to target legibility. Do not infer a carrier's private
  intention from the token alone; inspect expectations, assignment, refusal,
  sanctions, claimed jurisdiction, and collective effects.
- Analyze enforcement and exclusion as dual operations without treating them as
  identical in every case: identify the conformity condition, the standing or
  domain withheld after refusal, the applicable jurisdiction, and who observes the
  consequence.
- Treat private, collective, ideological-form, and fundamentalist as ideal-type
  propagation and enforcement configurations. Keep *Beseelung*, recruitment,
  retention, jealousy, jurisdiction, sanctions, and physical violence as separate
  axes.
- When order level matters, ask whether participants share concrete rules and
  answers or the generator that produces and corrects them. Treat dense detailed
  rules as evidence, and pluralism as a possible output, rather than either as the
  definition.
- State which parts come from the theory, which case facts require external
  historical or empirical support, and which conclusions are application-level
  inferences.
- In historical cases, separate structural genesis, articulation, sacralization,
  institutionalization, and later reproduction. Distinguish endogenous mythology
  formation from evidence of purposive construction; do not infer an engineer from
  cultural fit or infer control from an engineer's intention. Also separate
  historical cause, present affective classification, and avowed reason.
- Use full human-readable document and section titles so the analysis can be
  audited. Treat prompt logs as provenance, not theory authority or evidence.
- Preserve vivid originating terms, judgments, examples, and analogies while
  identifying their status and limits. An example illustrates a mechanism; it does
  not establish a population-level generalization.

## Common failure modes

- **Decorative use:** theory terms appear, but a generic explanation supplies all
  causal work.
- **Unitary-mind reduction:** the local bigOther is collapsed into cognition, bias,
  conformity, or internalized belief.
- **Methodological-individualist reduction:** the collective field is collapsed
  into an aggregate of separately formed persons and loses its back-action.
- **Hierarchy-of-identities substitution:** Christian, national, professional, or
  familial material is modelled as independent bigOthers or governing programs
  ranked by a higher runtime evaluator. Apply single-slot exclusivity instead:
  compatible packages form one weave; incompatible capture requires rejection or
  unbraid/rebraid replacement. Do not describe a counterfield as a second card.
- **Identity-label proof:** a claim to be “both” traditions is treated as proof of
  parallel evaluators without testing camouflage, reinterpretation, syncretic
  successor formation, liminal instability, or final interpretive priority.
- **Resonance inflation:** every revolution, crisis, cascade, effervescence, or
  severe outcome is called a *Resonanzkatastrophe* without evidence of prepared
  receivers, recurrent mode selection, reciprocal feedback, common knowledge,
  nonlinear amplification, and macroscopic reorganization.
- **Formation--construction conflation:** distributed cultural growth is described
  as though one actor designed it, or deliberate target-state intervention is
  dissolved into anonymous emergence.
- **Sovereign-engineering inference:** a declared destination, message-testing
  apparatus, or phrase such as *memetic engineering* is treated as proof of
  control. Compare intention with the successor actually selected by receivers,
  institutions, rivals, consequences, and reality.
- **Freedom-as-empty-slot:** emancipation is described as escape from programming,
  weaker capture, or loss of binding rules. It is rebraiding from shared concrete
  answers into one fully installed answer-generating meta-program.
- **Pluralism-as-definition:** lifted solidarity is defined by diverse ways of life
  or bounded institutions. Define it by the shared answer generator; plurality is
  one possible generated result.
- **Lifted-authority localization:** the bounded jurisdiction of parliament, a
  court, an office, a profession, or another representative is mistaken for a
  bounded lifted order. The meta-order universally constitutes and limits these
  plural authorities.
- **Rivalry-as-pathology:** replacement is condemned merely because the successor
  is rival. Diagnose the actual failure: closure, uncorrectability, interpretive
  monopoly, epistemic sovereignty, or jurisdictional spillover.
- **Context flooding:** every source is loaded even when the core answers the
  question, making peripheral similarities compete with the relevant mechanism.
- **False authority:** a proposed project construct is stated as established
  scholarly consensus, or an external source is made to support more than it does.
- **Authorship bleaching:** distinctive claims or analogies are softened into an
  anonymous institutional voice instead of being stated and then qualified.
