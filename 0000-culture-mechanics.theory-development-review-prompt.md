# Culture Mechanics — End-of-Conversation Theory-Development Review Prompt

**Status:** reusable terminal prompt; non-authoritative

Paste the prompt below near the end of a conversation in which Culture Mechanics
was applied, criticized, compared, or refined. In a very long conversation, it can
also be used for intermediate checkpoints. It is not a theory source or a
persistent instruction file.

## Prompt

Now review this conversation from the earliest content still available through the
latest message. Produce a theory-development inventory of the key items that might
help to develop Culture Mechanics further. Do not produce only a smooth narrative
summary.

Do not bleach elements merely because they are controversial, rhetorically
forceful, politically charged, speculative, or inconsistent with a neutral
academic register. Preserve the originating formulation before identifying its
epistemic status, evidential burden, boundaries, alternatives, and possible tests.
Do not silently replace the author's judgments with generic model-generated
consensus prose.

State an originating claim, verdict, hypothesis, or analogy clearly and memorably
before framing it:

> **State the dangerous claim first. Put the epistemic frame around it, not over
> it.**

Treat statements offered as *Zeitzeugnis* first as situated testimony in their own
terms, without silently translating them through present moral filters:

> **A Zeitzeuge is not a calibrated instrument, but situatedness creates access.**

Preserve what was said before distinguishing situated access, memory,
interpretation, accuracy, representativeness, corroboration, and generalization.
Preservation is not endorsement, and testimony is not by itself population-level
or causal proof.

Organize the inventory under these headings:

1. Originating claims, verdicts, terms, and memorable formulations.
2. Candidate definitions, distinctions, relationships, and causal mechanisms.
3. Application findings that may expose a general mechanism or boundary.
4. Counterexamples, tensions, contradictions, and rival interpretations.
5. Analogies, including their useful and unlicensed inferences.
6. External observations, evidence, authors, works, or source leads requiring
   verification.
7. Evidential burdens, discriminating questions, possible tests, and falsifiers.
8. Authoritative documents and cross-references each candidate might affect.
9. Open questions and deliberately deferred material.
10. Material omissions or earlier conversation content that cannot be recovered
    with confidence.

For every material item, identify:

- the originating formulation or a close, clearly marked paraphrase;
- whether it came from the user, the assistant, an attached source, or an external
  source discussed in the conversation;
- its proposed contribution to the theory;
- its epistemic type and current status;
- supporting considerations and missing evidence;
- boundaries, alternatives, counterexamples, and possible tests;
- its likely semantic home and any documents it would affect.

For a proposed constitutional classification orientation or new pole, identify the
independent governing question, unit and scale, endpoint definitions, observable
evidence, boundary cases, and interaction with the existing truth and drive
orientations. Test whether it is genuinely independent rather than a mechanism,
outcome, system state, or near-synonym already represented within an existing
orientation. Do not combine orientations into one scalar moral score merely
because a case can be located on several of them.

For a proposed **Ragnarök trajectory**, separate short-run propagation success
from long-run system valence. Identify the alleged terminal organizing logic, the
capabilities and carrying substrate it recruits, the way maintenance or
transmission serves that logic, the loss of credible stopping conditions or a
habitable successor, accumulated substrate damage, and possible interruption.
Do not infer the trajectory from anti-futurist language, conflict, catastrophe,
or eventual failure alone. Compare ordinary collapse, bounded destructive
transition within a life-drive project, antagonistic over-coupling, and
anti-futurist self-consumption as alternatives or routes.

For Culture Program material, audit candidates against *Culture Program Ecology*:
first identify whether a candidate belongs to analytical typology or mechanism,
problem and hazard diagnosis, or normative solution design. Do not make
acceptance of one layer automatically entail the next or treat the candidate
pluralist design as the only possible or already proven solution. Then
keep Culture Strategy versus Pure-Ideology separate from order level,
private/collective/ideological/fundamentalist form, corrigibility, jurisdiction,
*Beseelung*, entrusted power, Culture Program capture, capture susceptibility,
and hazard. Preserve legacy terms before proposing migration, and distinguish a
program, component, mixed braid, descendant, carrier, institution, population,
and state response. For capture candidates, separate disagreement, program
disharmony, objective substitution, system-level capture, coordinated-operation
hypotheses, and actor-specific covert-command attribution; identify the proposed
response and whether its evidential burden is proportionate. For early-warning
or countermeasure candidates, distinguish protected human carriers from the
Culture Program carrying structure; identify the exact finance, formation,
coordination, amplification, protection, logistics, or succession function, its
causal nexus to the hazard, affected person-level rights, less restrictive
alternatives, review, stop conditions, and repair after error.

For self-report, self-description, self-statement, or proclamation candidates,
preserve and state the originating words first. Apply the candidate
Self-Attribution Presumption: initially believe an authenticated, serious
proclamation as evidence of the actor's own operative allegiance, motivation, or
perceived authorization and as a program-level warning. Then distinguish that
first-pass credence from the truth of the reported cosmology, parent-program
causation, agreement by other carriers, upstream command, and individual legal
responsibility. Identify timing, authenticity, standing, script fit, internal
contestation, organizational support, recurrence, affirmative defeaters, and the
response the evidence could justify.

For hostile third-party attribution, apply the Attribution-Source Asymmetry:
record the accusation and its evidence separately from the target's
self-description. Nuclear taboo vocabulary such as “Nazi” or “Cultural WMD”
does not bootstrap target classification. If projection, Accusation in a Mirror,
the projective-vandalism circuit, or denunciatory jouissance is proposed, preserve
the originating “externalize → strike → enjoy” sequence but require independent
evidence about accuser and target, distinguish observable operation from
motivational hypothesis, and test strategic signaling, anger, conformity,
platform incentives, and accurate accusation as alternatives.

For secret-program or public–internal-divergence candidates, apply the
Propagation–Visibility Constraint and audit public doctrine, recruitment,
internal and elite formation, spontaneous member descriptions, action grammar,
sanctions, institutions, and offspring. Treat secrecy as a predicted
scale/fidelity trade-off, not an impossibility theorem or proof of public candour.

For systemic-skeleton/living-cultural-body candidates, preserve the originating
body analogy before testing it. Do not turn the two mutually dependent
implementation layers into two Culture Programs, two branch types, or two
bigOthers. Distinguish a program's rules, offices, jurisdictions, incentives,
sanctions, feedback, accounting, correction, and succession from its human
carriers, mythology, meaning, sacredness, identity, affect, moral formation,
*Beseelung*, and embodied habits. Ask whether the candidate explains durability,
coordination, succession, animation, or capture, and whether “skeleton” has been
confused with the narrower Culture Program carrying structure.

For adaptive-constitutional, SGD, scorecard, local-experiment, soft-secession,
reversed-legal-priority, or foot-voting candidates, preserve the originating
proposal and then separate: the common person-protecting constraint shell; the
space of permitted variation; proposal generation; scorecard, audit,
argumentative, revealed-choice, and foot-voting sensors; selection and correction;
and the living moral formation that operates the system. Identify whether the
claim belongs to the generic anti-gnostic controller in *Epistemic Constitution*
or its pluralist application in *Culture Program Ecology*. Treat SGD as a
controlled analogy, scorecards as legible but gameable sensors, and migration as a
costly aggregate valuation of a jurisdictional bundle rather than a truth oracle
or causal decomposition. Staying is not consent. Test mobility constraints,
selection and exclusion, subsidies, externalized costs, lag, common-mode error,
rights floors, cross-boundary obligations, appeal, rollback, and whether
jurisdictional diversity preserves or defeats the shared lifted order. Record the
legal and historical genealogy of *umgekehrte Maßgeblichkeit* as a source lead
requiring verification rather than as proof supplied by the theory.

For Lifted-Order Capacity Dashboard candidates, preserve its mixed provenance:
the assistant proposed **Speak · Own · Refuse · Exit · Appeal · Correct ·
Cooperate · Build · Choose**, and the user selected it for integration. Keep the
nine capacities separate from one another, from the definition of lifting, and
from a scalar Supreme Good. Audit their grouping, construct validity, sensor
independence, distributions, and conflicts among experienced,
administrative/material, and revealed-behaviour evidence. For *Own*, preserve the
user's yeoman-farmer claim that a homestead is literal personal safe space and a
material anchor, then distinguish homestead stock, generation, and retention;
nominal title from equity, control, utility access, sustainable carrying costs,
and security against arbitrary dispossession; and state hollowing from ordinary
taxation, legitimate externality control, infrastructure financing, creditor
risk, and voluntary high-cost location. Treat sentinel failures as proposed
non-compensable constitutional floors, not as self-authenticating allegations.

For democracy-as-*Schutzbalken* candidates, preserve three source layers:
Mausfeld's externally attributed account of democracy as an egalitarian
protection against the destructive potential of power; the user's memorable
compression **“The essence of democracy is *Schutzbalken gegen die Macht*”**;
and the project's protected-object hierarchy, public/private power taxonomy,
bounded-protector recursion, institutional mapping, and dashboard
reinterpretation. Do not silently attribute the latter synthesis to Mausfeld.
Identify the concrete person or standing protected; the power centre and its
mechanism; jurisdiction, answerability, review, correction, succession,
alternatives, refusal, and exit; and whether the claimed guardrail actually
protects. Keep state coercion, majority pressure, corporate or creditor leverage,
platform and professional gatekeeping, and cultural authority analytically
distinct. Treat elections and other familiar machinery as corrigible
implementations rather than either the sufficient definition of democracy or
irrelevant decoration. Test the public protector against the same principle it
enforces, and distinguish legitimate bounded authority from domination.

For constitutional-risk, *ideologische Macht*, *Machtpolitik*,
*Willkürherrschaft*, Cipolla--Bonhoeffer--Normopathie--ponerology, AI
queen-bee, or Ideological-Power Hazard Diagnostic candidates, start with Appendix
A of *Culture Program Ecology*. Preserve five categories:
ideological-power capability; capture or exploitation as a conversion mechanism;
*Machtpolitik* as a possible objective; *Willkürherrschaft* as a possible
endpoint; and *Schutzbalken* as defensive architecture. Do not turn
*Machtpolitik* into a third constitutional orientation. Keep the truth and drive
orientations, subjecthood absorption, Babelization, corrigibility, jurisdiction,
carrying capacity, common-mode exposure, and protective readiness as independent
inputs to a typed hazard profile rather than one scalar score. The project
broadens Mausfeld's domination-oriented source concept into a general cultural
capability; do not attribute that extension to him. Treat scenario-level
precaution as possible support for low-regret architecture, not as proof of
conspiracy, design, intent, guilt, pathocracy, or a causal nexus sufficient for
coercion. Treat the AI queen bee as a speculative stress test and apply the
bounded-protector recursion to every proposed defence.

For the originating *woke*--*postmodernism*--*critical constructivism*
candidate application, preserve the umbrella equation as the author's
hypothesis rather than scholarly consensus or the generic diagnostic's
definition. Separate the social-mediation thesis, political-constitution thesis,
and reconstruction mandate. Identify the specified text, implementation,
institution, jurisdiction, period, and carrying structure; test
reality-answerability, reciprocity, internal contestation, constructive
succession, subjecthood protection, counter-cohesion, and alternative
explanations. Kincheloe is a primary affirmative source for critical
constructivism; Hicks supplies a strongly critical secondary interpretation.
Do not classify a population, infer motive or diagnosis from affiliation, or
allow the candidate case to make the diagnostic self-confirming.

For secession, reversed-priority, Landkreis, or distributed-security candidates,
preserve Ludwig von Mises as a co-origin of territorial self-determination while
keeping soft secession, reversed legal priority, SGD, and capture-resistant
jurisdictional topology project-specific. State the originating claim **“Soft
secession can be implemented with a stroke of the pen: change legal priority,
not physical borders”** before qualifying it: formal legal leverage does not
remove constitutional, jurisdictional, fiscal, infrastructural, rights,
externality, transition, or appeal work. For the security mapping, identify the
central prize, compromise path, blast radius, local failure domains, common-mode
dependencies, surviving alternatives, and recovery path. Test whether local
authority, personnel, finance, records, sensors, software, and correction are
genuinely independent. Do not turn decentralization into capture immunity or
attribute the project mechanism to Mises.

Do not silently turn an assistant suggestion into the originating author's claim.
Do not treat this conversation or this review as authority. End with a short list
of the highest-value candidates for human review, without automatically accepting
them into the theory.
